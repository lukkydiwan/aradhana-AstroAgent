# Astrology Reference Notes — Aradhana AstroAgent

## The Planets

### Sun
The Sun represents the core self, ego, identity, and life force. It shows who we are becoming, our purpose, and our conscious will. The Sun rules Leo and the 5th house. Themes: vitality, creativity, leadership, self-expression, father figures. The house and sign of the Sun reveal where we shine and seek recognition.

### Moon
The Moon governs emotions, instincts, memory, habit, and the subconscious. It reflects our inner world and emotional needs. The Moon rules Cancer and the 4th house. Themes: nurturing, home, the mother, emotional security, cycles, dreams. The Moon sign reveals how we feel and what makes us feel safe.

### Mercury
Mercury rules the mind, communication, logic, and short journeys. It governs how we think and speak. Mercury rules Gemini and Virgo, and the 3rd and 6th houses. Themes: language, analysis, curiosity, writing, commerce, siblings. When retrograde, Mercury invites review of communication and plans — not ideal for signing contracts or launching new projects.

### Venus
Venus governs love, beauty, pleasure, art, and values. It shows what and who we are attracted to. Venus rules Taurus and Libra, and the 2nd and 7th houses. Themes: relationships, aesthetics, money, harmony, sensuality, creativity. The Venus sign reveals our relationship style and what we find beautiful.

### Mars
Mars rules action, drive, desire, courage, and conflict. It shows how we assert ourselves and pursue what we want. Mars rules Aries and co-rules Scorpio. Themes: ambition, passion, anger, sexuality, competition, physical energy. The Mars sign reveals how we fight for what we want.

### Jupiter
Jupiter expands, brings optimism, wisdom, and abundance. It shows where we are lucky and where we grow through generosity and faith. Jupiter rules Sagittarius and Pisces. Themes: philosophy, travel, higher education, spirituality, law, expansion. The Jupiter sign shows how we seek meaning and where blessings come.

### Saturn
Saturn teaches through discipline, responsibility, and limitation. It represents karma, time, maturity, and hard-won mastery. Saturn rules Capricorn and Aquarius. Themes: structure, authority, lessons, career, aging, boundaries. The house Saturn occupies shows where we face our greatest challenges — and greatest long-term rewards.

### Uranus
Uranus governs revolution, sudden change, innovation, and individuality. It disrupts the status quo to make way for evolution. Uranus rules Aquarius. Themes: rebellion, technology, freedom, breakthroughs, humanitarian ideals. Uranus transits mark periods of unexpected upheaval and awakening.

### Neptune
Neptune dissolves boundaries, connecting us to spirit, dreams, illusions, and the collective unconscious. Neptune rules Pisces. Themes: mysticism, compassion, creativity, deception, addiction, selfless service, film, music. Neptune transits can dissolve old structures or create confusion and yearning for transcendence.

### Pluto
Pluto rules transformation, power, death and rebirth, and the unconscious. It governs what must be released for evolution. Pluto rules Scorpio. Themes: shadow, secrets, trauma, regeneration, obsession, control, profound change. Pluto transits mark generational turning points and deep personal metamorphosis.

### North Node (True Node)
The North Node shows the soul's evolutionary direction — the qualities to develop in this lifetime. It is unfamiliar, sometimes uncomfortable, but deeply fulfilling. Opposite the South Node, which represents ingrained past patterns.

### Chiron
Chiron is the "Wounded Healer." It marks our deepest wound and our greatest gift — we often teach what we most need to learn. Chiron's sign and house show the nature of the wound and the healing path.

## The Zodiac Signs

### Aries (March 21 – April 19) — Fire, Cardinal, Ruled by Mars
Bold, pioneering, courageous, impulsive. Aries energy initiates. Themes: independence, courage, assertiveness, new beginnings. Shadow: aggression, impatience.

### Taurus (April 20 – May 20) — Earth, Fixed, Ruled by Venus
Steadfast, sensual, patient, possessive. Taurus energy sustains. Themes: security, beauty, material comfort, loyalty. Shadow: stubbornness, resistance to change.

### Gemini (May 21 – June 20) — Air, Mutable, Ruled by Mercury
Curious, adaptable, communicative, restless. Gemini energy connects. Themes: information, duality, wit, social ease. Shadow: inconsistency, superficiality.

### Cancer (June 21 – July 22) — Water, Cardinal, Ruled by Moon
Nurturing, intuitive, protective, moody. Cancer energy protects. Themes: home, family, emotional depth, memory. Shadow: clinginess, fear of rejection.

### Leo (July 23 – August 22) — Fire, Fixed, Ruled by Sun
Generous, dramatic, creative, proud. Leo energy radiates. Themes: self-expression, leadership, performance, romance. Shadow: ego, need for approval.

### Virgo (August 23 – September 22) — Earth, Mutable, Ruled by Mercury
Analytical, service-oriented, precise, critical. Virgo energy refines. Themes: health, craft, discernment, helpfulness. Shadow: perfectionism, anxiety.

### Libra (September 23 – October 22) — Air, Cardinal, Ruled by Venus
Harmonious, diplomatic, fair-minded, indecisive. Libra energy balances. Themes: relationships, justice, aesthetics, partnership. Shadow: people-pleasing, avoidance of conflict.

### Scorpio (October 23 – November 21) — Water, Fixed, Ruled by Pluto/Mars
Intense, perceptive, transformative, secretive. Scorpio energy penetrates. Themes: power, intimacy, regeneration, the hidden. Shadow: control, jealousy, vengeance.

### Sagittarius (November 22 – December 21) — Fire, Mutable, Ruled by Jupiter
Optimistic, adventurous, philosophical, blunt. Sagittarius energy explores. Themes: truth, freedom, higher learning, travel. Shadow: excess, tactlessness.

### Capricorn (December 22 – January 19) — Earth, Cardinal, Ruled by Saturn
Ambitious, disciplined, pragmatic, reserved. Capricorn energy builds. Themes: achievement, structure, responsibility, legacy. Shadow: coldness, workaholism.

### Aquarius (January 20 – February 18) — Air, Fixed, Ruled by Uranus/Saturn
Visionary, humanitarian, unconventional, detached. Aquarius energy innovates. Themes: community, progress, originality, ideals. Shadow: aloofness, rebellion for its own sake.

### Pisces (February 19 – March 20) — Water, Mutable, Ruled by Neptune/Jupiter
Compassionate, intuitive, imaginative, boundaryless. Pisces energy transcends. Themes: spirit, empathy, art, dissolution, healing. Shadow: escapism, victimhood.

## The Houses

### 1st House — Self, Identity, Appearance
The Ascendant and 1st house describe the physical body, persona, and how the world first perceives you. The planet here colors your outward manner strongly.

### 2nd House — Resources, Values, Self-Worth
Money, possessions, and what you value. Also self-esteem and how you earn. Planets here affect financial approach and sense of security.

### 3rd House — Communication, Siblings, Local Travel
The thinking mind, speaking, writing, and early education. Short trips, siblings, and the immediate environment.

### 4th House — Home, Roots, Inner Foundation
Family of origin, the private self, ancestry, real estate. The IC (Imum Coeli) cusp. Planets here deeply affect your sense of home and belonging.

### 5th House — Creativity, Play, Romance, Children
Joy, self-expression, love affairs, artistic output, and children. Planets here flavor how you play and create.

### 6th House — Health, Service, Daily Routine
Work environment, physical health habits, pets, and acts of service. Planets here affect daily routines and how you serve others.

### 7th House — Partnerships, Marriage
The Descendant. Long-term partnerships, marriage, and significant others — as well as open enemies. Planets here shape relationship patterns.

### 8th House — Transformation, Shared Resources, Death
Inheritances, sexuality, taxes, joint finances, the occult, and psychological depth. A house of profound change.

### 9th House — Philosophy, Higher Learning, Long Journeys
Beliefs, religion, higher education, foreign travel, and publishing. Planets here shape worldview and the search for meaning.

### 10th House — Career, Public Life, Reputation
The Midheaven (MC). Vocation, ambition, public reputation, and the father or authority figure. Planets here color professional life.

### 11th House — Community, Hopes, Social Networks
Friendships, groups, aspirations, and collective causes. Planets here affect social life and long-term visions.

### 12th House — Hidden Realms, Solitude, Karma
The unconscious, hidden strengths and vulnerabilities, spiritual retreat, and what is hidden from view. Planets here operate beneath the surface.

## Retrogrades

When a planet appears to move backward from Earth's perspective, it turns inward. Mercury retrograde (3x/year, ~3 weeks) invites review of communications and contracts. Venus retrograde (every 18 months) surfaces relationship themes. Mars retrograde slows action and turns energy inward. Outer planet retrogrades are long and generational.

## Important Dignities

- **Domicile**: Planet in the sign it rules — strong and comfortable.
- **Exaltation**: Planet in its sign of peak power (Sun in Aries, Moon in Taurus, etc.).
- **Detriment**: Planet opposite its domicile — challenged expression.
- **Fall**: Planet opposite its exaltation — most weakly placed.

## Guardrails & Ethics

Astrology offers a symbolic map for self-reflection and timing awareness. It is not:
- Medical diagnosis or treatment advice
- Legal counsel
- Financial advice or investment prediction
- Fate — free will always operates within the chart's patterns

Always encourage seekers to consult licensed professionals for health, legal, and financial matters. Present readings as possibilities and invitations, not certainties.

## Moon Phases and Their Energies

- **New Moon**: Planting seeds, setting intentions, beginnings
- **Waxing Crescent**: Building momentum, taking first steps
- **First Quarter**: Overcoming obstacles, taking action
- **Waxing Gibbous**: Refinement, adjustment, perseverance
- **Full Moon**: Culmination, illumination, release, heightened emotions
- **Waning Gibbous**: Gratitude, sharing wisdom, integration
- **Last Quarter**: Releasing what no longer serves, letting go
- **Waning Crescent**: Rest, surrender, preparation for the new cycle
